history

Hyderabad, founded in 1591 by Muhammad Quli Qutb Shah, has a rich heritage that blends various cultures and influences. Originally a center for the diamond and pearl trade, it became the capital of the Nizams, who were known for their opulence and patronage of arts and architecture.

Geography

Located in the Deccan Plateau, Hyderabad is characterized by its rocky terrain and artificial lakes. The city has a tropical climate, with the best times to visit being from October to March when temperatures are mild.

Demographics

"Hyderabad is one of the most diverse cities in India, with a population of over 10 million. The primary languages spoken are Telugu and Urdu, along with English, reflecting its multicultural fabric."

Secreterate

A secretariate is an administrative unit that manages records and performs other secretarial duties. It can also refer to the office or staff that performs these duties.

Golconda Fort

Golconda was the principal capital of the Qutub Shahi kings. The inner fort contains ruins of palaces, mosques and a hill top pavilion, which rises about 130 meters high and gives a bird's eye view of other buildings. Golconda fort is undoubtedly one of most magnificent fortress complexes in India.

Nehru Park

Nehru Zoological Park is a must see for nature and wild life lovers. It also has rare species animals and birds, most of which are houses on conditions resembling their natural habitats as much as possible.
